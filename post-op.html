GLOWRIFY · YOUR WHITENING AFTERCARE

What to do after

Teeth whitening appointment

01

For the first 4 hours

For best results, have no foods or drinks except for water for 4 hours after your teeth whitening appointment.

4 HOURS
02

Avoid foods and drinks that stain

Avoid all foods and drinks that will stain your teeth:

  • × Coffee
  • × Tea
  • × Red wine
  • × Soy sauce
  • × Berries
03

Skip smoking and vaping

Avoid smoking or vaping for 24 hours.

24 HOURS
04

Brush on the first night

Brush your teeth with toothpaste and salt for the first night.
