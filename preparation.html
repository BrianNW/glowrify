GLOWRIFY · YOUR WHITENING PREP

How to prepare

Before your teeth whitening appointment

01

Brush and floss beforehand

Please brush and floss your teeth prior to your appointment.

02

Consider a professional cleaning

Professional cleaning is recommended prior to your appointment for better results.

03

If you have sensitive teeth

If you have sensitive teeth, please use sensitive toothpaste a couple days before your scheduled appointment.

04

Pregnancy or breastfeeding

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, please consult with your doctor before setting up an appointment.

05

Existing dental work

Dentures, crowns, fillings, veneers, implants, or other restorative treatment will not be whitened.
