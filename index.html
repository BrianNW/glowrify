Ask me anything ↗
How does teeth whitening work?

Whitening options vary. During a consultation, we can discuss in-office and take-home approaches, how they work, and what may suit your smile and goals.

Can whitening cause tooth sensitivity?

Some people experience temporary sensitivity. Tell us about any concerns during your consultation so we can discuss whether whitening is suitable and what to expect.

How white will my teeth get?

Results vary from person to person and depend on factors such as your natural tooth shade and the type of discolouration. We’ll talk through realistic expectations before you choose.

Is whitening right for everyone?

Not always. A consultation can help identify whether whitening is appropriate for you and whether existing dental work or other factors may affect the outcome.

Should I choose in-office or take-home whitening?

Both options have different considerations. We can explain the available approaches, how to use products as directed, and which option may fit your goals.

Where are you located?

You’ll find the studio in Surrey, BC, Canada. Once your visit is booked, I’ll send over the address and everything you need for an easy arrival.
