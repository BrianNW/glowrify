What is professional teeth whitening?

Professional teeth whitening uses a higher-strength bleaching system under professional guidance to safely lift stains from the enamel and brighten your smile more effectively and consistently than many over-the-counter products.

What types of stains can teeth whitening address?

Whitening is most effective for surface stains caused by coffee, tea, red wine, smoking, and natural darkening over time. It may not fully change discoloration caused by trauma, medication, or dental restorations, so a consultation helps determine the best option for your smile.

Can everyone have their teeth whitened?

Most people can be candidates for whitening, but not everyone is a perfect fit. Sensitivity, gum concerns, pregnancy, certain dental conditions, and existing crowns or veneers may affect the treatment plan, which is why a personalized consultation matters.

Will whitening change the color of crowns, veneers, or fillings?

Whitening products generally do not change the color of crowns, veneers, or composite fillings because those materials are not the same as natural tooth enamel. If you have restorations, we can help plan for a balanced, natural result.

How can I care for my smile after whitening?

To protect your results, avoid highly pigmented foods and drinks for the first 24–48 hours, use a straw when possible, brush gently, and maintain good oral hygiene. If you’re sensitive, use a desensitizing toothpaste and follow the aftercare guidance provided during your appointment.
